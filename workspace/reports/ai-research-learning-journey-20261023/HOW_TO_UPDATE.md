# Add the next learning milestone

Preserve the detailed run journal and raw artifacts first. Then update this readable account at a completed, reviewable decision; a submission receipt is a pending experiment, not a result.

For each new AI milestone, write one short explanation with:

1. **The concept:** Define a new term in plain English before using its acronym. Explain where it sits between prompt, GPU work, transfer and generated tokens.
2. **The question:** What prior result or failure made this worth investigating?
3. **The controlled change:** What changed, what stayed fixed, which hardware/job IDs were involved, and what useful outcome was required before spending more?
4. **The observation:** Actual values with units, errors and request counts, or the first concrete failure. Separate scheduler state, evidence validity and scientific interpretation.
5. **The lesson:** What did the evidence support, what did it not establish, and why retain, revise, repeat or close the branch?
6. **The spoken version:** One or two sentences that explain the decision to an audience without listing folders or CLI commands.
7. **The evidence and next step:** Link the exact review and raw provenance, keep the original failure, and mark a future action pending until a definite receipt/result exists.

Keep H100/H200 plots separate. Keep fixed official workload and quality gates visible. Explain protocol changes when showing historical score milestones. Do not turn diagnostic kernel shares, acceptance length, a single tiny gain or local exact IDs into official accuracy or a speedup claim.

Update SPEAKER_NOTES when the conclusion changes and GLOSSARY when a new concept becomes necessary. Keep dated historical evidence intact.
