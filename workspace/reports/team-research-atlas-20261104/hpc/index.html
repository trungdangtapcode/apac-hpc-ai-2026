A faster arm is not
a matched improvement.

Keep the CPU platforms separate. NSCC and Iris have different hardware, rank counts and experimental conditions. Do not rank them by raw seconds per step.

Distinguish a screen from a full run. The 14-step Iris minimum is an isolated observation. It is not a matched win against separate 40-step arms. The NSCC organizer-recipe reproduction ran the full 150 steps.

Read the decision, including losing branches. Our graph-placement and axis-order trials did not establish a stable matched win. The teammate recipe has a qualified short-run signal; the independent same-horizon ABBA repeat is 6.10% slower and rejects a stable preparation-only gain. Favorable historical observations remain separate.
