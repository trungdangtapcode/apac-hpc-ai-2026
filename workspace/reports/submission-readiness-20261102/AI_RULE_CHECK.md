# AI submission rule check

Checked 2 November 2026 against the freshly fetched official rules. This is a static package audit, not a deployment or organiser verification. Rule snapshot hashes and fetch time are in [official-rules/source.json](official-rules/source.json).

## Verdict

The packaged Slurm entry statically matches the official workload and has a fully specified 16-GPU PD topology. The rules explicitly allow configuring or patching any serving stack in Basic if the patch does not change the computation and is submitted as text. The NIXL patch therefore is **not automatically Bonus**: its eligibility depends on whether its descriptor-only change preserves the computation; judges review the submitted patch. The team has CPU geometry-equivalence evidence and actual GPU P/D warmup plus benchmark evidence, but not a controlled no-patch comparison for the same topology or the official ≥99%-reference accuracy result. The patched deployment is eligible to be presented as Basic on current evidence, subject to the organizer's review and quality check; do not claim the patch's independent speedup or proven causality.

## Required actions before claiming fully ready

1. Keep claims for the NIXL patch within the evidence: CPU range equivalence plus actual GPU P/D operation are supported; causal improvement and official quality are not. If presenting it as Bonus, provide the Q2 accuracy and matched same-platform speedup evidence.
2. Treat dedicated-account accessibility as an evaluator reconstruction check, not as a local-path packaging defect. The current offline preflight was on a neutral copied path; access under the actual judge identity and its asset mounts remains untested.
3. A complete post-reader-fix GPU replay is not represented by 27152; that job checked the readers on retained data only. Earlier GPU inference/client work in 27136/27145 remains valid evidence while the original scheduler outcomes remain FAILED.
