# A readiness failure led to a completely accepted portable AI release

**Date and reason.** 2026-11-02T08:57:13.375119+07:00, Vietnam time (UTC+7). The owner authorised a complete packaged-entry run after result-reader fixes. The question was deliverable reproducibility, not another speed sweep.

**Prior evidence and hypothesis.** Portable jobs 27136 and 27145 completed real inference and client work but failed separate reader namespace and alias paths. CPU job 27152 validated repaired readers on saved data, without proving a fresh GPU allocation. The exact-ZIP retry 28194 then failed before scoring: health returned HTTP 200 before asynchronous model registration, and `/v1/models` returned 503. All workers had become ready. The hypothesis was narrow: health alone was an insufficient readiness predicate. Waiting for a valid registered `qwen` inventory, with worker-liveness checks and the existing fixed deadline, should permit the unchanged official path to start while rejecting persistent failure.

**Change and control.** R2 changes only deployment readiness and its checksum in the 26-file frozen payload. Five local fixtures cover recovery, timeout, expired deadline and dead workers. Model, worker, client, reader and semantic code remain unchanged, as do two nodes, 16 H200 GPUs and N64. This is debugging qualification, not a matched throughput treatment. Original 28194 remains FAILED with no benchmark score.

**Actual acceptance and measurements.** Slurm 28197 completed 0:0 in 611 seconds, from 08:06:04 to 08:16:15 Vietnam time on 2 November. Valid gateway inventory appeared by 333 seconds; both semantic routes passed by 375 seconds; the official client, summary and performance gate completed by 603 seconds; cleanup became terminal. Exported goodput 15.257590866518365 requests/s multiplied by 200 gives **3051.518173303673 good output tokens/s**. Independent replay of 2856 raw rows separates 949 warmup from 1907 scored requests: **1881 passed both limits (98.63660199265862%)**, with 26 TTFT misses, zero mean-ITL misses and zero errors/cancellations. TTFT mean/p95 is 1100.315/1285.996 ms; request-mean ITL mean/p95 is 15.010/15.186 ms. The scored window is 120 seconds, not the whole 611-second allocation. All 26 submitted file hashes match the frozen payload.

**Inspection and limits.** Raw profiles, phase metadata, semantic responses and receipts, worker/router/Slurm logs and runtime batch/queue/KV samples were inspected. Initial pair-router 503 retries recovered before scoring and are not scored request errors. The 191 decode samples show 2–56 running requests and sampled queue zero; hardware GPU/CPU/network telemetry was not collected. Those samples do not establish a causal compute or queueing bottleneck. Client-retokenized output lengths of 199–201 had no reader mismatch; authoritative generated token IDs were not retained, so a physical truncation explanation is not established. Only two startup semantic checks ran here. This is not a new smoke-8/retrieval-12 suite, official quality evaluation or statistical reliability study.

**Assessment.** The acceptance reproduces the operating level of the historical 3044.400475 mean and separate native job 26766 at 3045.501509. Former complete cache peak 27378 at 3048.446289 is historical; its small treatment gain still did not justify promotion. Failed-allocation recovered observation 27145 at 3055.330517 remains a different result class. Lower valid replay 26313 at 2830.923547 remains unexplained. Do not call 28197 a matched optimisation gain or pool H100 and H200 results. Official full-set quality against the organiser reference remains UNKNOWN.

**Cost and decision.** Actual top-level use was 2.715556 GPU-hours for 28197 plus 1.484444 for failed 28194: 4.2 GPU-hours total, zero PBS SU. Reservations are retired. Accept the tested readiness correction and close the narrow final-replay objective without a new GPU trial. Broad research and autopilot remain stopped. Publish the tested script plus README/checksum metadata, current English report and speaking script, source-bound slide/guide/PDF artifacts and compact raw evidence.
