# Research milestone — 25 October 2026: bounded H200 closed-loop load sweep

**Question and prior evidence.** The modern H200 fast-default target-only control at N64 (job 25947) reached 2,757.36 good output tokens/s with 95.24% joint latency success in the short 20/40/30-second screen. A preceding short load screen from N64 to N68 recorded a 2.273457% throughput increase while passing its local gate. That preliminary result is unranked; it does not establish a full-protocol winner. The replicated full-protocol N64 frontier remains 3,044.400475 good output tokens/s.

**Hypothesis and change.** Closed-loop offered load may have headroom above N64. Four separate H200 jobs test N66, N68, N70 and N72. Each frozen bundle descends from the same S59 target-only fast-default payload. Concurrency is the only changed serving/workload variable. Model, BF16 weights/KV, P8-TP4/D8-TP8-EP8 topology, NIXL, chunk size, MoE/attention/allreduce backends, seed 2026, 4,000 input and 200 output tokens, and 20-second warmup / 40-second measurement / 30-second grace are held fixed. The actual concurrent jobs and scheduler receipts are recorded in the follow-up admission entry.

**Control and value.** Job 25947 is the modern N64 short control, but it is not contemporaneous. The sweep is grounded in the observed N64→N68 short improvement and answers where this fixed runtime crosses its latency limit. Each job uses 16 H200 GPUs across two nodes for at most 17 minutes. The queue cap permits no more than five pending/running jobs per account; one separate topology job, 26057, already occupies a slot. This wave uses the remaining four positions. H200 Slurm does not draw from the PBS SU ledger.

**Gate and limits.** Each point must independently reach at least 90% joint request success (TTFT ≤3,000 ms and mean ITL ≤20 ms under the AIPerf goodput definition), zero profiled request errors/cancellations, and the existing 7/8 output-smoke and 12/12 retrieval checks. A gate failure closes that point and does not invalidate lower loads. Compare good output tokens/s and request-level TTFT/ITL distributions; do not infer official accuracy from smoke/retrieval. This is an unranked short screen, not an official score or full-protocol confirmation. Do not submit higher load beyond N72 without inspecting these results.

**Next decision.** Retain the highest passing point only as a candidate for an independent longer confirmation. If every point is below the 3% improvement threshold over the contemporaneous N64 screen or fails a gate, keep N64 and close this load branch. Preserve each terminal failure and raw export. Official full-set accuracy remains unknown.

**Preservation.** The four configurations are separate immutable bundles with complete SHA256 manifests and exact S59 parent references. The source/preparation checkpoint is captured before scheduler submission; a separate receipt checkpoint records definite job IDs and live states.
