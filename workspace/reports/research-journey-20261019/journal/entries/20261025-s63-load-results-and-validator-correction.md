# Research milestone — 25 October 2026: close the N66–N72 load branch

**Question and prior evidence.** Modern H200 fast-default N64 short control 25947 produced 2,757.36 good output tokens/s and 95.24% joint SLA success. A prior short N64→N68 screen had suggested a 2.27% gain, so four one-factor loads were tested at N66, N68, N70 and N72. S62r2 job 26064 separately explored two TP4/EP4 decode replicas.

**Change and control.** Jobs 26065–26068 each used the fixed S59 target-only topology, 16 H200 GPUs, 4,000 input / 200 output tokens, seed 2026, and the 20/40/30-second short client protocol; only AIPerf concurrency changed. The matched reference is N64 job 25947, although it was not contemporaneous. Job 26064 used S62r2's four-worker, two-decode-replica topology and did not reach benchmark scoring.

**Correction: the scheduler state hid two checker defects.** Slurm marked 26065–26068 `FAILED 1:0` because our independent performance checker still required N64 and paths under `screen/n64`, even though the benchmark runner correctly measured the configured N. I did not modify the submitted copies. A separate audit now checks the raw configured-N summary, exact AIPerf input config, full per-request denominator and TTFT/ITL thresholds, good-request count, Goodput units, output responses and 12 retrieval results. It independently confirms the three lower loads as valid local short screens and N72 as a real SLA failure. Keep Slurm `FAILED` as the accurate execution state; the first three scores are recovered, verified short-screen evidence rather than official ranking results.

Job 26064 started all four workers and generated 32 before/after geometry receipts, then failed before route warmup/AIPerf because `validate_kv_geometry.py` referenced undefined `UNKNOWN_D_HEADS_ROLE`. After correcting the name, the whitelist also had to recognize the actually observed TP4 decode geometry; its old exception covered TP8 only. A corrected validator in immutable derivative S62r3 now permits only decode TP4/TP8 under strict rank, 188-region VRAM, 256-byte stride, before/after and registration checks. It passes all 32 retained 26064 records. It leaves head count, dtype, layer IDs and semantic order explicitly unknown.

**Results and units.** Good output tokens/s is AIPerf Goodput in requests/s multiplied by the fixed 200 output tokens. Jobs 26065/26066/26067 scored 2,533.33 / 2,465.71 / 2,497.14 tokens/s and achieved 94.29% / 91.36% / 90.75% joint success; each had zero profiling errors/cancellations, smoke 7/8, retrieval 12/12. Their short goodput deltas against 25947 were −8.12% / −10.58% / −9.44%. Job 26068 at N72 scored 2,269.48 tokens/s and only 82.33% joint success, below the 90% rule. All had mean ITL 14.39–14.54 ms and mean TTFT 1,816–2,045 ms. Per-request p95 TTFT was above 3,000 ms for the N66–N72 loads; up to 9.25% of requests can breach and the joint fraction still pass. This is why the fraction, not p95 alone, determines the local gate.

**Observability.** The independent review inspected 16 distinct GPU UUIDs and phase-bounded telemetry: mean sampled GPU active time 64.7–67.2%, VRAM allocation about 129.6 GiB/GPU, host CPU-jiffy busy estimates 12.2–12.3% on g4 and 14.3–14.7% on g1. In successful server-metric polls, exposed rank-local decode queue maximum was zero; sampled prefill rank-local queue maxima were 13–25 and rose with N. Decode `num_running_reqs` on its exposed rank-0 series peaked at 49–52. Server scrapes and gauges are sparse and rank-labelled, so they are not cluster-wide totals or proof that prefill queueing caused TTFT misses. ITL stayed well inside its threshold. See the review for per-job tails, sample coverage, CPU/memory limits and raw artifact paths.

**Assessment and next decision.** Close the concurrency increase branch: every passing tested point underperformed matched short N64, while N72 also failed SLA. None displaces the 3,044.400475 good-output-token/s long-protocol H200 frontier. Smoke/retrieval checks are not official accuracy verification. Re-submit one S62r3 job: it preserves the topology hypothesis while fixing the observed geometry-checker defect. Do not fill the queue with new N66–N72 repeats.
