# From coefficient search to a measured bottleneck hypothesis

The owner asked to think about improvements. We replayed 12 historical arms and 39 source hashes: repeated controls vary strongly; endpoint memory, paging and NUMA counters do not establish a causal bottleneck. The new controlled xyz/Scotch screen will add equal bounded in-solver sampling, preserve its directional status and native gates, and keep the existing 1,009-hour internal stage. No new admission is claimed here.

The prioritized next optimization hypotheses are 48-versus-56 rank density, verified memory locality, direct decomposition selection, then a profile-directed build or MPI change. Each is conditional on evidence and separately frozen; there is no authorized blind sweep.
