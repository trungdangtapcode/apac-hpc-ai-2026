# 24 October: decode-only EAGLE3 run exposes role-specific KV geometry

**Question and prior evidence.** The 20/40/30-second N64 pair 25947/25948 showed that EAGLE3 on every role reduced ITL but missed the joint 90% SLA gate. Native arguments on the H200 full-load target-only incumbent showed speculative decoding disabled on both prefill roles and decode. S61 tested EAGLE3 only on decode-0, leaving both prefill roles target-only. The hypothesis was that this would preserve decode-side ITL gains while avoiding draft-side prefill work and recovering TTFT. The full-protocol frontier remained 3,044.400475 good output tokens/s.

**Change and control.** Job 25988 used two H200 nodes and 16 GPUs: two TP4 prefill workers and one TP8/EP8 decode worker, N64, 4,000/200 tokens, seed 2026, cold prefix cache, with a 20/40/30-second short protocol. The matched short control was actual target-only job 25947. EAGLE3 was enabled only on decode-0 with two steps, top-k 1 and three draft tokens. The job had a 17-minute maximum and no PBS SU charge.

**Failure, not a score.** Slurm ended 25988 as `FAILED 1:0` after 6:15. All three workers passed startup health. Draft fingerprint and fast-runtime readiness passed. The geometry validator then failed before semantic P/D warmup or AIPerf: `empty KV layer IDs require exactly 190 pinned MHA KV regions`. There is no candidate score, request fraction, TTFT, ITL, request error count or output-sanity result for 25988.

**Observed geometry and correction.** The retained 32 receipts contain before/after records for 16 model ranks. Each of the eight target-only prefill ranks has 188 KV regions; each of the eight EAGLE3 decode ranks has 190. The latter matches 188 target-model registrations plus two additional draft registrations per rank. The old validator incorrectly applied 190 to every role because its previous EAGLE3 trial enabled draft on all workers. A corrected role-aware validator was replayed against the captured raw 25988 receipts and accepted all 32 records. Empty `kv_layer_ids` remains an explicit limit: semantic layer order is not observed.

**Assessment.** This is a validator bug exposed by the mixed-role experiment, not evidence of a bad KV allocation and not a performance result. Runtime evidence supports that target-only prefill and decode-only draft arguments were applied; the P/D semantic path and candidate performance remain untested. No official accuracy claim follows. Keep 3,044.400475 good output tokens/s as the H200 full-protocol frontier.

**Beyond the score.** Collected and inspected: job state/elapsed time, startup phase markers, draft identity, resolved runtime flags, per-role BF16 KV/NIXL record counts, and raw KV geometry. Resource sampler files were retained but not used to claim a bottleneck. No AIPerf profile means no TTFT tail, ITL, queue/inflight, request-error or goodput analysis. No kernel profiler was run. Reservation was 16 GPUs for at most 17 minutes; actual scheduler elapsed was 6:15, not PBS SU.

**Next decision.** Preserve 25988 unchanged as a failure. Repair the validator to require 188 target regions on target-only prefill and 190 regions on draft-enabled decode, replay it against all 32 raw records, freeze a new bundle, then retry the same short comparison if the fresh Slurm queue allows. A retry result is required before accepting or closing the decode-only performance hypothesis.
