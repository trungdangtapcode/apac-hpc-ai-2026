# Research milestone — actual date and descriptive title

Write the entry when the milestone becomes reviewable. Link it from the journey or job index and preserve the original source records. Label a later reconstruction as retrospective; never fabricate the original date or rationale.

**Question and prior evidence.** Explain the observed problem, relevant completed runs/profile and what remains uncertain. Separate a hypothesis from an established result.

**Change and control.** Name the platform/GPU, job IDs, frozen bundle, exact changed factor, matched control and settings held constant. Explain why this test answers the question and why it merits the time/credit. Do not pool H100/H200 scores.

**Measurement or failure.** Record terminal state separately from evidence validity. Link raw requests/exports, logs, seals, payload hashes, runtime checks and output diagnostics. An allocation failure, truncated benchmark or pending plan is not a passing measurement.

**Results with units.** Report good output tokens/s, request-level joint success fraction, TTFT in ms, request-mean ITL in ms, errors/cancellations and the scored protocol. Keep startup duration, GPU kernel sums and profiler traffic separate. Record actual charge and maximum reservation distinctly when relevant.

**Assessment.** Compare with the appropriate control/repeats, preserve tradeoffs and negative outcomes, and explain the limit of the evidence. A single small gain is not established causality or global optimality. Local output checks do not certify official accuracy.

**Next decision.** State retain, repeat, refine, close or defer, why, and the next bounded test/stopping rule. Closing a branch does not close the whole research programme. Do not claim submitted work before a definite scheduler receipt.

**Correction, if any.** Identify the earlier claim and record, what evidence changed the interpretation, and which conclusion now applies. Preserve old raw evidence and mark superseded prose historical.

**Preservation.** Capture only relevant safe active-source changes, verify, make a small descriptive commit and push to the private origin. Link recovery/object metadata for external artifacts; a Git push does not upload those bytes. Do not retain authentication material.
