## Runtime budget and cost ceiling

The prior full chain took about 1,134 seconds wrapper time with one 549-second solver. Four such solver runs imply approximately **2,781 seconds (46.4 minutes)** before the extra arm copies, per-arm mapping probes, and expanded validation; this is a planning extrapolation, not a measured estimate. A 45-minute request is already shorter than that rough four-run estimate. Recommend a **60-minute PBS walltime maximum** (512 cores ×1 hour ×1 SU/core-hour = **512 SU maximum requested allowance**), with a 57-minute internal deadline and three-minute cleanup margin. Check quota/space before copying and use explicit per-phase deadlines. If setup or copies consume the margin, stop before launching partial solver arms.
