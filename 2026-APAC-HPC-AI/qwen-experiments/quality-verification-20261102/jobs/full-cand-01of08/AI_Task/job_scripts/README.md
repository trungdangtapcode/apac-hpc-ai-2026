Derived shard 1 of 8 evaluates 1,504 rows from the prepared 12,032-question MMLU-Pro corpus at concurrency 64. It preserves the accepted candidate model, worker flags, precision, topology, NIXL patch, prompt construction, greedy decoding, 2,048-token output limit and scorer. This is internal quality evidence, not BTC certification or ranked AIPerf.

A corresponding reference shard uses the same question IDs. Aggregate correct counts over the fixed 12,032-question denominator; do not average shard ratios.
